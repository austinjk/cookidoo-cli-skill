# Austin's TM7 accessories

Reviewed against official Vorwerk and Cookidoo material on 2026-08-04. Treat these accessories as available but optional:

- Thermomix® TM7 Cutter+
- Thermomix® Blade Cover & Peeler
- Thermomix® Sensor
- Thermomix® TM7 Nester

Prefer the simplest reliable workflow. Use an accessory only when it materially reduces prep, improves uniformity or presentation, protects delicate food, improves doneness monitoring, or provides useful individual portions. If an accessory is used, name it in the visible recipe step and include the required setup, cut/mode, quantity or batch limit, and external appliance. My Creations cannot reliably activate accessory modes, so use prose-only manual handoffs rather than promising an automatic Start button.

## TM7 Cutter+

The TM7-only Cutter+ makes six cuts: thin slices (1–2 mm), thick slices (4–5 mm), thin grate (2 mm), thick grate (4 mm), thin spirals, and thick spirals. It is useful for uniform gratins, salads, slaws, rösti, pickles, fruit preparations, vegetable noodles, and garnishes. Cookidoo examples include potato gratin, ratatouille galette, carrot spiral pickles, and zucchini tagliatelle.

Use it when uniformity or volume makes hand cutting meaningfully slower. Do not force it into a recipe for one small ingredient or an intentionally rustic cut.

- Prefer raw, firm produce. Cooked and frozen ingredients are unsuitable for slicing or grating except cooked beetroot.
- Use thick slicing for juicy ingredients; thin slicing or grating tomatoes, melon, kiwi, and similar produce performs poorly.
- Keep each slicing/grating batch within the approximately 800 g cutter-basket capacity.
- For spirals, use firm, dense produce with stones, seeds, and cores removed. Prepare straight pieces 4–10/11 cm long and 3–5 cm in diameter, then center them on the blade.
- State thin/thick plus slice/grate/spiral explicitly. Tell the cook to fit the correct Cutter+ assembly and select the matching function manually.

## Blade Cover & Peeler

This accessory covers the mixing knife while allowing circulation, protecting delicate ingredients or sous-vide bags during liquid cooking. Its rough surface also peels raw root vegetables.

Use the peeler for a useful batch of potatoes, carrots, beetroot, or turnips when it saves hands-on prep. For peeling:

- Process one vegetable type at a time, 200–800 g, cut to even sizes when necessary.
- Add 600 g water; optional oil may reduce foaming.
- Select Peeler manually. The official baseline is 4 minutes/speed 4, clockwise, without heat, but firmness may require 30 seconds to 6 minutes. Prefer the on-device Peeler flow and inspect the result rather than promising perfect peeling.

Use the blade-cover function for a verified Slow Cook or Sous-vide workflow, or for delicate pieces that need liquid circulation without blade contact:

- Keep food and liquid below the 2.2 L maximum. Sous-vide bags must remain fully submerged without exceeding that mark.
- Use bags rated for the exact sous-vide time and temperature. Follow the official/source recipe's water, acid, time, temperature, and bag instructions.
- Preserve the named Slow Cook or Sous-vide mode as a manual handoff. Do not infer settings or replace the mode with ordinary TTS.
- Do not use the blade cover above 100°C.

## Thermomix Sensor

The Bluetooth Sensor measures food core temperature and ambient temperature for cooking outside the mixing bowl: oven, pan/hob, barbecue/grill, or air fryer. Cookidoo uses it for meat, poultry, fish, bread, and cake recipes. French examples include roast chicken, turkey fillet en croûte, breads, yogurt cake, and chocolate fondants.

Use it when core temperature is a better endpoint than a fixed time, especially for thick or variable-size meat and fish, whole poultry, bread, and cake. Do not add it to short, predictable cooking where it creates more setup than value.

- In a created recipe, tell the cook to open Sensor in the TM7 Cooking Center or Cooking Center app and set the verified target manually. Do not imply My Creations can launch an integrated Sensor program.
- Obtain target core temperature from the exact official/source recipe or a current authoritative food-safety source. Never invent or casually approximate it.
- Insert the probe's internal sensor near the food's core and insert the reference notch as directed. Meat and fish portions should be at least 2 cm thick.
- Avoid bone; use the holder for soft cake batter and keep the tip from touching the pan.
- Keep the charger near the probe but away from direct heat. The probe limits are 100°C internal and 275°C ambient.
- Never use the Sensor in a microwave or pressure cooker.

## TM7 Nester

The TM7-only Nester is a food-safe silicone mould with eight 95 ml cavities shaped for the TM7 Varoma tray. It supports steaming, oven baking, proofing, chilling, and freezing and is rated from -60°C to +230°C. Cookidoo examples include eight poached eggs, mini omelettes/frittatas, steamed buns, egg muffins, small cakes and puddings, gelées, parfaits, and mini cheesecakes.

Use it when eight neat individual portions, hands-off Varoma steaming, or easy mould release improves the recipe. Do not force a recipe into the Nester when a single large item or a different vessel is simpler.

- Keep each portion within a 95 ml cavity and state how many cavities to grease or fill.
- For Varoma cooking, explicitly tell the cook to place the filled Nester in the TM7 Varoma tray and preserve the verified water, Steam setting, and time.
- Distinguish Varoma steaming from external oven baking and from refrigerator/freezer setting. The TM7 itself does not bake or freeze.
- Avoid sharp knives, metal utensils, and abrasive cleaning tools on the silicone.
